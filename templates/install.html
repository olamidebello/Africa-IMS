Install on your phone

  1. Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow app installation for the browser you used.
  3. Tap Install, then Open. Sign in to your AIMS account.

Keep Android security checks enabled. This is a direct APK download, not a Google Play listing.

The app opens the AIMS website in a dedicated HTTPS window. It includes Home, Reload, Back, a PDF/image document picker and external browser support. Downloads may require signing in again in your browser. Offline records, native biometrics and push notifications are not included.
